## See your cloud clearly.

Lake Ave Labs is an engineering consultancy based in Orlando, Florida. We work across cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, identity and native iOS apps.

### What we do

- **Cloud audits:** Read-only reviews of AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and Cloudflare environments, with written, risk-ranked findings.
- **Platform engineering:** DevOps, CI/CD and Kubernetes platforms that help teams ship safely and consistently.
- **Identity and security:** Okta, Microsoft Entra ID and Intune, designed around least privilege and Zero Trust.
- **iOS development:** Native iPhone and iPad apps built with Swift and SwiftUI, from technical scoping through integrations, testing and App Store release.

### About the company

Lake Ave Labs LLC is a Florida limited liability company founded in 2024. Our approach is calm, precise engineering: understand the environment, explain the tradeoffs and make the next step clear.
